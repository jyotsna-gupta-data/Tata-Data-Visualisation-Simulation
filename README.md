# Tata Group Data Visualisation & Executive Insights Simulation 📊
![Download Preview](Screenshot%202026-09-29%20230142.png)

## Executive Summary
Completed a virtual job simulation with **Tata Group** on Forage, building visual dashboards for senior leadership (CEO & CMO). Cleaned and analyzed online store sales data to help the business track revenue, find new markets for growth, and understand seasonal shopping trends.

## Tools & Methodology
- **Data Analytics & Visualization:** Power BI (DAX, Interactive Dashboards, Trend Analysis) / Tableau
- **Data Preparation:** Data Cleaning, Removing Negative Values, Outlier Filtering, Regional Grouping
- **Methodology:** Executive Reporting, Revenue Growth Analysis, Customer Mapping

## Key Findings & Deliverables
- **Data Cleaning:** Removed invalid sales records (like negative prices and quantities) to make sure all financial reports were accurate.
- **Sales & Revenue Drivers:** Identified best-selling global markets and peak shopping months to help with stock and supply planning.
- **Market Expansion Strategy:** Analyzed high-revenue countries (outside the UK) to highlight top locations for business growth.
- **Customer Insights:** Found top-buying customer groups to help the marketing team plan targeted ad campaigns.
